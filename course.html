ACADEMIC CURRICULUM 2025

What Really Happened

Understanding Complex PTSD Through Lived Experience & Clinical Rigor

The Wounded Blueprint Course

A 16-lecture series designed as a Harvard-style seminar. This curriculum explores the clinical architecture of trauma using From the Storm to the Fire as a primary case study. Each lecture pairs lived experience with peer-reviewed research.

PART I: FOUNDATIONS OF TRAUMA
Lecture 01

The Truth Behind the Silence

Analysis of the initial fracture and the sociopolitical factors of trauma suppression.

Lecture 02

The Masks We Wear

Understanding adaptive personalities and the survival utility of the false self.

Lecture 03

The Glass Box Blueprint

NICU trauma, early nervous system wiring, and epigenetic inheritance before language.

Lecture 04

Wired for War

Neuroception, hypervigilance, and the brainstem threat response as adaptive genius.

PART II: RELATIONSHIPS & REALITY
Lecture 05

When Love Hurts

The neurobiology of attachment in high-stress or abusive environments.

Lecture 06

The War on Reality

Gaslighting, cognitive dissonance, and the systematic dismantling of the victim’s agency.

Lecture 07

Trauma Bonding

Why the nervous system encodes the abuser as a lifeline—and what it takes to rewire.

Lecture 08

The Fawn Response

Appeasement as survival: people-pleasing, disappearing, and rebuilding identity after.

PART III: INTERNAL LANDSCAPE
Lecture 09

Shame vs. Responsibility

Deconstructing the “Internalized Aggressor” and the burden of unearned guilt.

Lecture 10

Internal Family Systems

Meeting your parts: the Protector, the Exile, and the Caretaker still fighting the original war.

Lecture 11

Temporal Dysregulation

Why trauma lives in the “now” and the challenge of establishing a linear future.

Lecture 12

The Body Keeps Score

Somatic memory, chronic pain, and befriending the body that protected you.

PART IV: COLLAPSE & EMERGENCE
Lecture 13

The Dark Night of the Soul

Necessary deconstruction: when the armor fails and what lives underneath.

Lecture 14

Rage as Sacred Energy

Reclaiming suppressed anger as information, boundary signal, and creative fuel.

Lecture 15

Radical Acceptance

The difference between forgiveness, surrender, and genuine peace with the unchangeable.

Lecture 16

Post-Traumatic Growth

Identity beyond survival: who you become when the emergency is finally over.
