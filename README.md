**26 Laws of Survival: A clinical map of Complex PTSD drawn from lived experience. No coddling, no bullshit.**

Live site built on the frameworks from the memoir *From the Storm to the Fire: 26 Laws of Survival* by Daniel Lingar.

---

## What This Is

An interactive, multi-page educational website that translates the lived experience of Complex PTSD into clean frameworks, plain-language clinical terminology, and survival tools. The site is the clinical map. The memoir is the terrain.

---

## Site Structure

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Landing — The Wounded Blueprint homepage |
| `start-here.html` | Entry point for people new to CPTSD |
| `glass-box.html` | The Glass Box Blueprint — NICU origin trauma architecture |
| `the-26-laws.html` | Core framework — 26 Laws of Survival (Season 1) |
| `terminology.html` | Clinical glossary in plain English |
| `the-evidence.html` | Scientific and clinical support layer |
| `who-i-am.html` | Author identity and context |
| `resources.html` | Crisis hotlines, therapist directories, book launch updates |
| `course.html` | Harvard-style trauma curriculum for clinicians and educators |
